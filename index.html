Why they're famous

Nothing fancy. Just good ingredients, handled properly, the same way for more than sixty years.

Beef ground in house
100% Australian grass-fed beef, free of chemicals and hormones, from a family-run Sydney butcher that has been going for over 50 years. Ground fresh in the shop to Paul's 1957 recipe.
Pineapple crush squeezed daily
Queensland's sweetest pineapples, freshly squeezed every day. The drink people cross Sydney for.
Produce we pick ourselves
Lettuce, tomato and onion inspected and bought direct from Flemington Markets every week, from growers we've known for 20 years.
Sauces made here
Paul's special sauce is a rich, creamy aioli made to a secret recipe. The homemade hot chilli is a blend of fresh marinated chillies with real heat.
Traditional buns
Super soft, seeded, king-size buns. Ask for seedless if you prefer.

From a hole in the wall to a Sydney institution

  1. 1957

    Paul and Katina Zerefos buy a block at 12 Princes Highway and build a mini market. Katina has the builders leave a seven-foot opening to the street, and burgers are the only thing sold through it. Locals call it the hole in the wall. A hamburger costs one shilling and sixpence.

  2. 1983

    The queues win. The hole in the wall takes over the whole mini market and Paul's Famous Hamburgers is born.

  3. 1998

    After 41 years, the Zerefos family hands over to Nick and Sandra Sinesiou, who keep the recipe, the systems and the spotless benches, and extend the hours so nobody gets turned away.

  4. Today

    Same address, same recipe. Grab your burger and walk it down to the Georges River to eat under Tom Uglys Bridge, like people have for generations.

Did you know? Paul never made a hamburger himself. And there was no beetroot in 1957, because Katina found it stained her white benches.
